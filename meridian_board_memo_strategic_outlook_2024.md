# Meridian Board Memo — Strategic Outlook 2024

*From: Lenore Pak, CEO*
*To: Meridian Technologies Board of Directors*
*Date: October 14, 2024*
*Re: Q4 strategic outlook and 2025 planning*

---

This memo previews the strategic discussion for the Q4 2024 board meeting. It is
my candid view of where Meridian stands and what I believe we should do.

## Where we stand

Meridian will end 2024 at approximately **$360 million in revenue, growing
~16% year-over-year**. Operating margin will land near 10%, up materially
from the 4% we ran in 2022. We have generated approximately $60 million in
free cash flow this year. Cash on hand will exceed $430 million.

These are healthy numbers in absolute terms. They are not the numbers we
imagined when we set the 2023–2026 plan two years ago. The 2026 revenue
target was $560 million; my best estimate today is that we will end 2026
between $480 million and $520 million on the current trajectory.

## What is going right

1. **Enterprise is working.** Enterprise will end 2024 at ~$138M ARR, up
   from $74M two years ago. NRR holds above 125%. This segment is the asset.

2. **The cost discipline of the last eighteen months has been real.** S&M
   has come down from 38% to 34% of revenue. G&A from 12% to 10%. Operating
   margin expansion is on plan.

3. **Compliance work has paid off.** FedRAMP Moderate and HIPAA both
   shipped this year, and both have been cited in 23 enterprise wins.

## What is going wrong

1. **SMB has deteriorated more than the plan anticipated.** I projected a
   controlled wind-down. What is happening is closer to a hemorrhage. SMB
   ARR is down ~30% over 24 months and the rate is accelerating.

2. **AI Copilot is late and the gap to competitors is widening.** Copilot
   was promised for Q4 2023, then Q2 2024, and is now scheduled for closed
   beta this quarter and GA in Q1 or Q2 2025. Asana shipped their agent
   this fall. I do not believe we will catch up on a build path; we will
   need to consider acquisition.

3. **Mid-market is plateauing.** NRR has compressed from 115% to ~106%.
   Renewals are increasingly contentious. Customers are pushing on price
   and asking for AI features bundled in.

## Where I think we should go in 2025

Three big bets, in order of priority.

1. **Double down on enterprise.** Expand the enterprise sales team to 95
   reps. Launch GxP for pharma in Q3 2025. Sign a Tier 1 SI partnership in
   H1 2025. Set a 3-year target of $250M+ enterprise ARR.

2. **Get Copilot to GA in 2025 — and consider M&A to accelerate.** The
   internal build path will deliver GA in Q2 or Q3 2025. The M&A path
   could add another 12–18 months of compressed roadmap. I recommend
   actively exploring acquisitions in the $50M–$200M range targeting
   AI-native collaboration teams.

3. **Stop subsidizing SMB from enterprise economics.** Run SMB on a
   self-funded basis with its own P&L. Do not pursue any new SMB
   acquisition spend that does not pay back within twelve months. Manage
   the segment for cash, not for growth.

## On my own role

I have served as CEO since I co-founded the company. I want the board to
know that I believe Meridian's next chapter is best led by a CEO with deep
experience scaling a company through a major platform transition (in this
case, agentic AI). I am ready to support the board in finding that person
and to step into a different role at the right time. I would welcome a
private conversation on this.
