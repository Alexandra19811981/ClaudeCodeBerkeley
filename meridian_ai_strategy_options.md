# AI Strategy Options for Investor Day

*Internal — joint memo from CPO and CSO to the CEO*
*Date: February 22, 2026*

---

This memo lays out the two strategic positions in detail. Each has a
different sales motion, hiring mix, M&A pipeline, and 2027–2028 revenue
shape. Pick one.

## Option A — "PM Platform with AI"

**One-sentence positioning:** *Meridian is the project management platform
that enterprise governance teams trust, with industry-leading AI features
that make our PM motion faster.*

**Strategic logic.** We have a real, defensible asset: governance,
compliance, audit, role-based access. Asana doesn't have it. Monday
doesn't have it. The AI-native upstarts can't catch up to it in three
years because it's slow regulated-industry work. Lean into it. AI Copilot
is the upgrade SKU on top.

**What this commits us to:**

- **Pricing:** Per-seat (current model). Copilot is a per-seat add-on at
  $40/month. No consumption pricing.
- **Sales motion:** PM-led. Win on PM functionality, governance, vertical
  features. Copilot is the close-the-deal sweetener.
- **Hiring mix 2026:** ~70% sales/CS/SE roles, ~30% engineering. Hire 12
  vertical sales engineers (financial services, life sciences, federal).
- **M&A:** PM-adjacent — resource management, time tracking, compliance
  reporting. Avoid further AI-native acqui-hires in 2026; integrate Helio.
- **R&D priority:** Vertical depth (FedRAMP High, GxP), governance
  features, mobile, integrations. AI as feature, not platform.
- **2028 revenue shape:** ~$700M, low-teens growth, 18%+ operating margin.
  Boring is the goal.

**Risks:**

- The market may decide PM-with-AI is the loser's seat by 2027, and we
  are stuck on it.
- The Helio team did not join Meridian to build "AI features for PM." They
  joined to build an agent platform. Retention risk is high if we choose
  this path.
- Mid-market customers increasingly want agentic features, not PM-with-AI.
  This path concedes mid-market over time.

**Who internally wants this:** Sales, CS, the enterprise GM, the CFO.

## Option B — "Agentic Work Platform"

**One-sentence positioning:** *Meridian is the agentic work platform —
projects are how we started; agents are how work actually gets done in
the next decade — and we run on the most trusted enterprise infrastructure
in the category.*

**Strategic logic.** PM as a category will commoditize as agents become
the primary user interface. Whoever builds the platform on which enterprise
agents run wins. We have two real assets to combine: the Helio agent
framework and our enterprise governance moat. We are uniquely positioned
to be the *enterprise-grade* agentic platform.

**What this commits us to:**

- **Pricing:** Hybrid — per-seat for legacy PM, per-seat plus consumption
  for the agent platform. Consumption pricing announced at Investor Day,
  rolled out H2 2026.
- **Sales motion:** Agent-led for new logos and for net-new expansion in
  existing accounts. PM motion preserved for renewals and SMB.
- **Hiring mix 2026:** ~50% engineering (heavily applied AI), ~30%
  product, ~20% sales/CS. Hire 25 applied AI engineers, 8 agent product
  managers, 4 ML/safety engineers.
- **M&A:** AI-native acqui-hires, agent-builder marketplaces, possibly
  evaluation/observability tools for agents. Avoid PM-adjacent acquisitions.
- **R&D priority:** Agent governance (audit, role-based agent permissions),
  agent marketplace, agent evaluation, vertical agent libraries. PM
  receives maintenance investment only.
- **2028 revenue shape:** Wider range — could be $900M+ at 25% growth if
  it works, or $550M at 5% if it doesn't. Higher variance, higher upside.

**Risks:**

- We may underestimate how slowly enterprises adopt consumption pricing.
- The agent category may consolidate around the model labs (Anthropic,
  OpenAI) before we get to scale, and we end up as a thin enterprise wrapper.
- We deemphasize the PM motion that pays the bills today; if agentic
  revenue ramps slower than planned, we have an air gap to manage.

**Who internally wants this:** Engineering, product, the Helio team, the
applied-AI hires we made in Q4, parts of marketing.

## Cross-cutting questions both paths must answer

1. **What is our public posture toward Anthropic, OpenAI, and other model
   labs?** Partner, neutral platform, or competitor? The market will ask.
2. **What is our SMB story?** Both paths leave SMB underserved. Acceptable
   if we're explicit about it; not acceptable if we're vague.
3. **What is our governance story?** Our biggest asset is enterprise trust.
   How do we operationalize that into our AI / agent positioning?

We recommend the CEO take a clear position by March 1 so that internal
communications, IR, and the Investor Day materials team have nine days to
build around it.
