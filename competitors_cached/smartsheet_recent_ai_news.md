# Smartsheet — AI Posture Summary (cached)

*Cached snapshot, ~February 2026, for fallback use if WebFetch fails.*

## Headline positioning

Smartsheet positions itself as **"the enterprise work platform you can
trust with AI"** (Smartsheet investor presentations, Q4 2025 earnings;
recent press releases). Their AI offering is branded simply **"Smartsheet
AI."**

The positioning emphasizes **trust, governance, and enterprise control**
more heavily than any of the other three competitors. Smartsheet has
notably *not* used the word "agentic" in its primary marketing, instead
preferring "AI-augmented" and "AI-powered."

## Most recent flagship announcement

In **December 2025**, Smartsheet launched **"Smartsheet AI Compliance
Pack"** — a set of governance, audit, and access-control features
specifically targeted at AI usage inside the platform. Features include
agent-level audit logs, model selection controls, data residency
enforcement, and content filtering.

This was framed as a **defensive feature for regulated industries** rather
than a customer-acquisition feature. The pricing reflects that: it is a
premium add-on for enterprise customers only, priced at ~$15/seat/month
on top of existing licenses.

## Pricing posture

- Existing Smartsheet tiers: unchanged.
- **Smartsheet AI:** Bundled into Business and Enterprise tiers.
- **Smartsheet AI Compliance Pack:** Premium add-on, enterprise only.
- **No consumption pricing.**

## Strategic positioning

Smartsheet is the most aligned with **Meridian's Option A (PM platform
with AI features)**. Smartsheet has explicitly rejected the "agentic
platform" narrative and instead positioned around **enterprise trust,
compliance, and the boring fundamentals**.

Their CEO said publicly in November 2025: *"We are not in the race to
build the most ambitious agent. We are in the race to build the agent
that an audit committee can sign off on."*

## Likely place vs. Meridian's options

Smartsheet is the **closest direct comparable to Meridian's Option A**.
If Meridian picks Option A, Smartsheet is the obvious peer. The
positioning will be: "Meridian is what Smartsheet wants to be in
regulated verticals it does not yet serve." That is potentially a strong
story but also a story that has Meridian competing for a position
Smartsheet already holds.

If Meridian picks Option B, Smartsheet looks like the more cautious
peer. Meridian could position as "the enterprise governance company that
saw the agent shift earlier than Smartsheet did" — which is a credible
contrast.

## Open questions

- Smartsheet's growth has decelerated meaningfully in 2025 (commentary
  in their Q4 call suggested low-single-digit growth in 2026).
- Smartsheet has been the subject of ongoing acquisition rumors (private
  equity, strategic). Their share price reflects some takeout premium.
- Smartsheet has not announced a model partnership, although their
  Compliance Pack supports BYO-model.
