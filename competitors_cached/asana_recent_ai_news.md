## Headline positioning

Asana publicly positions itself as **"the work management platform with AI
built in"** (Asana CEO commentary, Q4 2025 earnings; Asana blog, January 2026).
Their AI offering is branded **"AI Studio"** and **"Smart Workflows."**

In their own words (paraphrased from recent investor-day materials):
*"Our customers don't want a separate AI tool. They want their work
management platform to think and act with them."*

## Most recent flagship announcement

In **November 2025**, Asana announced that their AI Studio and Smart
Workflows features would be **bundled into the Advanced and Enterprise
tiers** at no additional cost. Previously these had been a separate
add-on. The bundling was framed as removing a barrier to AI adoption.

This was a meaningful pricing escalation in the category and was
explicitly aimed at competitors (likely including Meridian) that still
charge for AI features as add-ons.

## Pricing posture

- **Standard tier:** No AI features.
- **Advanced+ tiers:** Smart Workflows and AI Studio bundled.
- **No consumption pricing announced.** Asana has explicitly said agent
  consumption pricing is "not the right model for our customers right now."

## Strategic positioning

Asana is closer to an **"agentic platform with PM as the wedge"** posture
than to "PM with AI features." Their Q4 2025 commentary repeatedly used
language about "Asana as an agent operating system" and de-emphasized
classic PM functionality in marketing materials.

## Likely place vs. Meridian's options

Asana is most aligned with **Meridian's Option B (Agentic Work Platform)**.
If Meridian picks Option A, Asana will look like the bolder, more
forward-leaning competitor. If Meridian picks Option B, Meridian's
differentiator vs. Asana has to be enterprise governance — Asana has not
emphasized governance / audit / role-based agent permissions to the same
degree.

## Open questions

- Asana has not publicly committed to a specific model lab (Anthropic,
  OpenAI, internal). Their materials are studiously model-agnostic.
- Asana has not yet announced an agent-builder marketplace, although the
  product roadmap hinted at one for "later in 2026."
- Asana's enterprise governance / audit features still lag Meridian's.
