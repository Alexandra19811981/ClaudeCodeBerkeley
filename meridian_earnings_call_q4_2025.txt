CATHERINE PARK
  Good afternoon. Q4 revenue one hundred three point two million, up
  ten percent. ARR closed the year at four hundred thirteen million,
  up ten percent. Full year revenue four hundred million, up eleven
  percent.

  Operating margin Q4 twelve point four percent. Free cash flow margin
  seventeen point eight percent. Cash and equivalents four hundred
  sixty-three million, after closing Helio Labs in November.

  This was my first full year as your CEO. Three things to discuss.

  First, the segment reorg is in production. Each of our three GMs ran
  their own P&L for Q4. Enterprise grew twenty-one percent ARR year over
  year. Mid-market grew six percent. SMB declined twenty-three percent.
  The shape of our business is now visible in a way it has never been.

  Second, AI Copilot. End of Q4 paying seats seven hundred ten, against
  a target we'd set internally of six hundred. Approximately three point
  five million in 2025 ARR contribution. Forty-four percent attach rate
  on enterprise renewals signed in Q4 — above our forty percent target.
  These are early but real proof points.

  Third, on Helio integration — we have moved roughly half of the Helio
  team into the Copilot product organization and half into a new
  agentic-experiences group reporting to me. Twenty-six of twenty-eight
  Helio employees are still with the company.

  For 2026 we are guiding to revenue of four hundred forty to four
  hundred fifty-five million, implying ten to fourteen percent growth.
  We are guiding operating margin of fourteen to fifteen percent. The
  range reflects continued AI investment offset by efficiency in mature
  segments.

  This is the year we have to decide what Meridian is. The board and I
  have been having that conversation. I will share my point of view at
  our Investor Day on March eleventh.

Q&A (excerpted)
================================================================================

ALLISON PARK / MORGAN STANLEY
  Catherine, the 2026 guidance — ten to fourteen percent. The Street was at
  fifteen. Walk us through it.

CATHERINE PARK
  Allison, three things. One, we are baking in continued SMB decline —
  about thirty million headwind to growth. Two, AI Copilot ramp is real
  but we are early; we are not modeling it as the dominant growth driver
  for this year. Three, we are continuing to be disciplined on price.
  We could chase fifteen percent by getting aggressive on enterprise
  discounting. We will not.

BRENT COSTA / GOLDMAN SACHS
  The Investor Day in March — what should we expect?

CATHERINE PARK
  A clear statement of where Meridian is going for the next three years
  and how we will get there. We will share new financial targets, our
  AI roadmap in detail, and the long-term strategic shape we believe
  in. I won't preview any of it today.

PRIYA RAMAN / JPMORGAN
  Capital allocation in 2026 — buyback or M&A?

CATHERINE PARK
  Likely both, weighted to M&A. We have run a careful screen. We have
  identified two additional acquisition targets in the AI-native space
  that we are in early diligence on. Nothing announced. The buyback
  resumes in Q2.

KARIM OUEDRAOGO / CITI
  Competitive update.

CATHERINE PARK
  Asana shipped their full agent suite in November and pricing pressure
  in mid-market is now the steady state. Monday continues to scale and
  is now larger than us by ARR. ClearAI Work raised a one hundred
  twenty million round at a one billion plus valuation; they remain a
  competitive thorn in SMB. Atlassian's announcement last week of an
  agentic Jira is the more interesting development — they will be in
  our enterprise deals more directly than they have been historically.

WENDY BAO / WELLS FARGO
  You said this is the year Meridian decides what it is. Can you say more?

CATHERINE PARK
  Wendy, the deeper question is whether we are still primarily a
  project management tool with AI bolted on, or whether we are an
  agentic work platform that happens to have project management as one
  of its surfaces. The answer to that question changes our R&D priorities,
  our sales motion, our pricing model, and our positioning. The board
  and I have a strong view. We will share it at Investor Day.
