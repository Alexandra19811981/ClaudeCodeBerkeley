Q&A (excerpted)
================================================================================

ALLISON PARK / MORGAN STANLEY
  Helio Labs — twenty-eight people for a seventy-eight million headline
  number. Walk us through it.

CATHERINE PARK
  Allison, I get the question. Three pieces. One, only ten million is
  cash at close — the other sixty-eight million is retention equity that
  vests over four years, the team has to stay for us to pay it out.
  Two, the engineering bench is the asset — twenty-eight engineers all
  from leading AI labs. Three, the agent framework Helio built compresses
  our roadmap by an estimated fifteen months. We modeled the build-cost
  of doing this in-house at one hundred ten million plus eighteen months
  of opportunity cost in a market where every quarter behind hurts. The
  structure was the right structure.

BRENT COSTA / GOLDMAN SACHS
  How are you measuring AI Copilot success?

CATHERINE PARK
  Three metrics, in order. One, attach rate to enterprise renewals — we
  want this above forty percent within twelve months of GA. Two, in-product
  usage of agentic actions — what percentage of our users use an AI
  feature weekly. Three, willingness to pay — does Copilot show up in NPS
  comments and does it appear in win-loss reasons. We will report these
  publicly starting Q1.

PRIYA RAMAN / JPMORGAN
  SMB — any update?

CATHERINE PARK
  SMB ARR was sixty-eight million Q4 last year. It will end this year
  closer to fifty-two million. Net retention in the low eighties. The
  reorg lets us run SMB self-funded. We are no longer chasing this
  segment with discounted enterprise economics.

KARIM OUEDRAOGO / CITI
  Asana bundled their AI into standard tier in October as they had
  signaled. Are you seeing pricing pressure?

CATHERINE PARK
  Yes. Specifically in mid-market renewals. We are seeing renewals push
  for at least one of: a price hold, expanded user counts at the same
  price, or AI Copilot bundled in at no cost. We are holding the line on
  Copilot pricing at forty per seat. We are giving on price holds where
  the customer is strategic.

WENDY BAO / WELLS FARGO
  How do you respond to the analyst notes that say Meridian is "structurally
  disadvantaged" against Asana given the scale gap?

CATHERINE PARK
  Wendy, two answers. The pessimist note is correct that Asana has a
  larger R&D budget and a bigger user base for training agentic features.
  The optimist note — which is mine — is that we win in regulated
  enterprises where Asana does not, and that segment values us more for
  governance and audit features than for raw AI horsepower. We will
  continue to invest there.
