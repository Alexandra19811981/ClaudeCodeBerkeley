# Meridian — 2025 Annual Employee Survey: Themes Summary

*Internal — People team summary for the CEO. Survey conducted October 2025.
Response rate: 81% (1,945 of 2,402 eligible employees).*

This is a thematic summary of the open-end responses, not the full survey.
Quantitative engagement scores are reported separately to the People &
Compensation Committee.

## Top themes (by frequency in open-ends)

### 1. "Roadmap thrash" — Engineering and Product

The single most common theme in open-end responses, mentioned in 31% of
engineering responses and 24% of product responses. Representative quotes
(anonymized):

- *"Three different AI roadmap pivots in eighteen months. Each pivot we
  burn weeks reorienting. The third pivot was the right one. Why didn't
  we get there in pivot one?"*
- *"I love the Copilot work. I am tired of the resource management work
  being deprioritized for it, then resurrected, then deprioritized again."*
- *"My team has shipped twice as much code in 2025 as in 2024. The amount
  of it that has actually reached customers is much less. Something is
  broken in how we plan."*

### 2. "We don't know what we are anymore" — Cross-functional

Mentioned in 19% of all responses, with the highest concentration in
sales (28%) and marketing (24%). Representative quotes:

- *"In sales pitches I cannot crisply say what makes Meridian different from
  Asana with AI. I used to be able to."*
- *"Are we a project management tool with AI features, or are we an AI
  platform with PM as one surface? The answer is different week to week."*
- *"The new positioning that came out in October was clearer than what we
  had. It also raised questions for half my customers about whether we are
  still committed to the things we sold them on."*

### 3. "I trust Catherine" — Leadership

Mentioned positively in 22% of responses across all functions. The new CEO
is broadly viewed as decisive, candid, and engaged. Cautions appear in a
smaller subset (8% of responses), expressing concern that:

- The pace of change is high and burnout risk is real.
- Several senior executives appointed before Catherine may not survive his
  first eighteen months — uncertainty is unsettling.

### 4. "Helio integration is going better than I feared" — Engineering

Mentioned in 14% of engineering responses. Generally positive. Some concern
about cultural fit and about whether Helio's "ship fast and break things"
norm will undermine Meridian's "ship safely" enterprise commitments.

### 5. "Pay is not keeping up" — Cross-functional, with concentration in
   senior engineering

Mentioned in 18% of responses overall, 27% in senior engineering. Specific
asks:

- Refresh equity grants for tenured employees whose grants vested before
  the 2025 stock decline.
- Re-benchmark senior engineering pay against AI-native startup
  compensation.
- Provide clearer career ladders, particularly for IC engineers above
  staff level.

### 6. "Customer Success is energized" — CS and renewals

Mentioned in 26% of CS responses. Largely positive — enterprise customers
are renewing and expanding, and the team feels the wind at their back.
Concern about losing experienced reps to competitors offering large
sign-on bonuses.

### 7. "What about international?" — EMEA team

Mentioned in 41% of EMEA responses, 9% of global responses. Concern that
the strategic conversation is North America-centric and that the EMEA
team is not being adequately resourced (sales engineers, localization,
customer success).

## Themes that did not appear (or were minor)

- **No significant volume of complaints about return-to-office, remote
  work, or hybrid policy.** This was the dominant theme in 2023 and 2024
  surveys; it has receded.
- **Diversity and inclusion** — fewer mentions than in prior years, with
  the few mentions evenly split between "we are doing well" and "we have
  stopped investing here."
- **Compensation transparency** — mentioned in fewer than 5% of responses,
  down from 14% in 2024.

## People-team recommendations to the CEO

1. **Communicate the strategic direction explicitly and stick to it.** The
   "roadmap thrash" theme will resolve only when the organization believes
   the current direction is durable.
2. **Refresh senior IC engineering compensation in Q1 2026.** Specific
   risk of losing 15–25 senior engineers in a tight market.
3. **Make a visible commitment to EMEA in the 2026 plan.** Either invest
   meaningfully or be honest that EMEA will not get materially more
   investment in 2026.
4. **Acknowledge the burnout risk.** A visible CEO statement on pace and
   priorities, with explicit permission to slow down on non-critical work,
   would be received well.
