# Internal Brief — Investor Day Positioning

*From: Priya Chen, Chief of Staff*

*Date: February 25, 2026*
*Subject: Setting the table for Investor Day positioning*

---

Catherine,

This memo lays out the binary positioning question we need to resolve before
Investor Day on March 11 and why we keep going in circles on it.

## The question

In one sentence to the market on March 11, what is Meridian for the next
three years?

The two camps inside the company:

- **The PM Camp.** Meridian is a project management platform. AI is a
  feature, used to make our PM tool the best PM tool. We compete with
  Asana, Monday, Smartsheet, Atlassian on PM depth, on enterprise
  governance, on workflow rigor. Copilot is a feature.

- **The Agentic Camp.** Meridian is an agentic work platform. PM is the
  surface we started with, but the platform's real value is the agent
  framework Helio brought us. We compete with — and may eventually be
  acquired by, or compete head-on with — the model labs (Anthropic, OpenAI)
  and the agent-native upstarts (ClearAI Work, Linear AI). Copilot is the
  strategy.

These are not technically incompatible (in product terms, you can do both).
They are incompatible in *positioning* terms, and positioning shapes
everything downstream:

| Implication | PM Camp | Agentic Camp |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing model | Per seat (today's model) | Per seat + agent consumption |
| Sales motion | PM-led; AI as upgrade | Agent-led; PM as wedge |
| Hiring priority | Enterprise sales engineers | Applied AI engineers, agent product managers |
| M&A pipeline | Adjacent collaboration tools | More AI-native acqui-hires |
| Most-comparable peers (in the market's eyes) | Asana, Monday, Smartsheet | Smaller AI-native companies, eventually labs |

## Why we keep going in circles

Three reasons.

1. **The leadership team is genuinely split.** Sales/CS lean PM (it's what
   they sell, it's what customers buy). Engineering and product lean
   agentic (it's what they're building, and what they think wins long term).
   You and I haven't picked a side and that ambiguity has cascaded.

2. **The data is genuinely ambiguous.** Enterprise customers are using
   Copilot but not because we're an agentic platform — they're using it
   because they trust our governance. SMB is dying to AI-native upstarts
   but the upstarts are mostly small. Mid-market is where the question is
   sharpest and the data is thinnest.

3. **The market hasn't decided yet either.** Asana and Monday have been
   shape-shifting their AI positioning quarterly. Atlassian's recent
   "agentic Jira" announcement is a real signal. Smartsheet has been quiet,
   which is itself a signal.
