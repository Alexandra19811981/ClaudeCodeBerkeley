# Meridian Technologies Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2024 Results

**Boulder, CO — January 30, 2025** — Meridian Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: MRDN),
the project management and team collaboration platform, today announced
financial results for the quarter and fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

## Fourth Quarter 2024 Highlights

- Revenue of $93.7 million, up 14% year-over-year
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) of $374.8 million, up 14% year-over-year
- Operating margin of 10.5%, up from 8.5% in the year-ago quarter
- Free cash flow margin of 16.8%
- Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $431.5 million

## Full Year 2024 Highlights

- Revenue of $360.2 million, up 16% year-over-year
- Operating margin of 9.95%, up from 7.18% in 2023
- Free cash flow of $58.5 million, a free cash flow margin of 16.2%

## CEO Transition

The company today separately announced that **Lenore Pak**, who has served as
Chief Executive Officer since the company's founding in 2014, will be
stepping down from the CEO role effective immediately. Ms. Pak will remain
on Meridian's Board of Directors. The Board has appointed lead director
**Aldous Renn** as interim CEO while a permanent successor is identified.

"It has been the privilege of my career to build Meridian alongside our
employees, customers, and partners," said Lenore Pak. "After eleven years,
the company is ready for the next chapter and I am ready to step into a
different kind of contribution. I have full confidence in the team and the
Board to write that next chapter."

"Lenore built one of the most respected collaboration software companies of
the last decade," said Aldous Renn. "On behalf of the Board, we thank her.
The Board is engaged in a thorough search for a CEO who can lead Meridian
into the agentic AI era, and we expect to make an announcement in the
coming weeks."

## 2025 Outlook

In light of the leadership transition, the company is providing only first
quarter 2025 guidance at this time, and will provide full year 2025
guidance at its next earnings call:

- First quarter 2025 revenue is expected to be in the range of $94 million
  to $98 million.
- First quarter 2025 operating margin is expected to be approximately 10%.

Full year 2025 guidance will be provided once the new CEO is in place and
has had time to engage with the business.

## About Meridian Technologies

Meridian Technologies is a project management and team collaboration
platform serving more than 21,000 customers worldwide, from small teams to
the world's largest enterprises. Headquartered in Boulder, Colorado, with
offices in London, Berlin, Amsterdam, and Toronto.
