# Meridian Technologies — Customer Segment Overview

*Internal — prepared for the new CEO, Q1 2025 onboarding pack.*

Meridian sells its project management and team collaboration platform across
three customer segments. As of Q4 2025, segment composition by ARR is:

| Segment | Definition | ARR (Q4 2025, $M) | YoY ARR growth | NRR (Q4 2025) | Gross logo churn (Q4 2025) |
|---------|------------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|----------------------------|
| Enterprise | >5,000 seats | ~$167M (~40% of ARR) | +21% | 125% | 2.6% |
| Mid-market | 500–5,000 seats | ~$194M (~47% of ARR) | +6% | 102% | 10.2% |
| SMB | <500 seats | ~$52M (~13% of ARR) | -23% | 84% | 22.1% |

For context: in Q4 2022, the mix looked very different — SMB was the largest
segment at roughly 38% of ARR, mid-market 35%, enterprise 27%. The enterprise
share has grown by roughly 13 points and SMB has shrunk by roughly 25 points
in three years.

## Enterprise (~40% of ARR, healthy)

- ~140 logos.
- Driven by displacement deals against Microsoft Project, legacy on-prem PPM
  tools, and (increasingly) Smartsheet at the high end.
- 6 of the top 10 retail banks in North America. 4 of the top 8 global
  pharma companies. Strong vertical penetration in financial services and
  life sciences.
- Average annual contract value: $295K.
- Sales cycle: 9–14 months.
- Implementation revenue (services) is roughly 18% of new enterprise ACV.
- This is where the company is winning. NRR 125%; expansion is reliable.

## Mid-market (~47% of ARR, plateauing)

- ~3,200 logos.
- Direct sales-led, supported by inside sales for renewals.
- Mostly mid-cap professional services, manufacturing, and tech.
- Average ACV: $42K.
- Net retention has compressed from ~115% (2022) to 102% (2025) as expansion
  slows. Most of the compression is from price-sensitive renewals at the
  lower end of the segment.

## SMB (~13% of ARR, in decline)

- ~18,400 logos.
- PLG (product-led growth) acquisition: free trial → self-serve checkout.
- Heavily exposed to two competitive threats:
  1. **Cheaper alternatives** — Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp, Notion at lower
     price points and with stronger free tiers.
  2. **AI-native upstarts** — ClearAI Work, Linear AI, and others promise
     "Claude-style" agentic assistants in-product. Meridian's own AI Copilot
     was in limited beta until Q3 2025.
- Net retention has collapsed from 115% (2022) to 84% (2025). Gross logo
  churn is 22% per year.
- SMB share of ARR has fallen from ~38% (2022) to ~13% (2025) — partly through
  losses, partly by design (the prior CEO had begun deliberately deprioritizing
  SMB acquisition spend in favor of enterprise).

## What this looks like in plain terms

- The enterprise business is doing the heavy lifting and growing.
- The mid-market is treading water.
- The SMB business is shrinking quickly. Two open questions for the new CEO:
  (1) is the SMB decline a controlled wind-down or an out-of-control hemorrhage,
  and (2) does Meridian's brand and PLG funnel still serve as a top-of-funnel
  for the rest of the business if SMB continues to contract?
