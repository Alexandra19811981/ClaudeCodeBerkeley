Q2 2025 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL

CORPORATE PARTICIPANTS
  Tara Linwood    Head of Investor Relations
  Catherine Park     President and Chief Executive Officer
  David Hoyt      Chief Financial Officer

================================================================================
PREPARED REMARKS (excerpted)
================================================================================

CATHERINE PARK
  Good afternoon. Q2 revenue ninety-eight point eight million, up eleven
  percent. ARR four hundred eight million. Operating margin twelve percent.

  Three updates from the strategic review I promised on the last call.

  One. We are reorganizing the company around three customer segments —
  enterprise, mid-market, and SMB — each with their own P&L starting Q4. We
  have been running one go-to-market motion for three very different
  customers and the math no longer works.

  Two. The AI Copilot moved from closed to open beta this quarter. Paying
  seats at quarter end were one hundred forty. That's small, but it is no
  longer zero. We are targeting general availability in Q3 with three
  enterprise lighthouse references.

  Three. On capital allocation, we are accelerating our buyback. The board
  authorized an additional one hundred million on top of the existing
  authorization. We continue to evaluate M&A in the AI-native space.

DAVID HOYT
  Subscription revenue ninety-three point two. Services five point six.
  Gross margin seventy-nine point five. Operating margin eleven point six,
  modestly ahead of our internal plan.

  Cash and equivalents four hundred forty-seven million. We repurchased
  forty million of stock during the quarter.

  We are tightening 2025 revenue guidance to three hundred ninety-eight to
  four hundred two million from three hundred ninety to four hundred five.
  Operating margin guidance unchanged at twelve to thirteen percent.

================================================================================
Q&A (excerpted)
================================================================================

ALLISON PARK / MORGAN STANLEY
  Catherine, on the segment reorg — does this mean you've decided to keep SMB?

CATHERINE PARK
  We've decided not to exit it abruptly. We've decided to stop subsidizing
  it from enterprise economics. SMB will run on a self-serve, fully-loaded
  basis. If it can fund itself, it stays. If not, we will manage it down
  in a controlled way over the next eighteen months.

BRENT COSTA / GOLDMAN SACHS
  AI Copilot — one hundred forty paying seats. What's the price point and
  what's the trajectory you'd want investors to model?

CATHERINE PARK
  Forty dollars per seat per month, as an add-on to existing licenses.
  Trajectory — I'd ask investors to model meaningful contribution starting
  in 2026, not 2025. We have to earn it.

PRIYA RAMAN / JPMORGAN
  M&A — anything you can share?

CATHERINE PARK
  Nothing to announce. We have an active list. We are looking specifically
  at AI-native collaboration tools where the engineering team and IP would
  meaningfully advance our roadmap. We have set an internal target deal
  size of fifty to two hundred fifty million.

KARIM OUEDRAOGO / CITI
  The competitive environment — has anything changed?

CATHERINE PARK
  Asana announced they are bundling their AI agent into the standard tier
  starting in Q4. That's a meaningful escalation. Monday continues to
  compete on price. There is a new entrant — ClearAI Work — they are
  small but the demos are good and they are taking some of our SMB.

WENDY BAO / WELLS FARGO
  Headcount and morale.

CATHERINE PARK
  Headcount roughly flat at twenty-four hundred employees. We did a
  modest workforce reduction in G&A of about eighty roles in May, which
  the company knows about. Morale — I'd say it's stabilizing. The
  segment reorg is energizing for some teams and disorienting for others.
  My CHRO and I have a lot of work ahead of us on culture.
