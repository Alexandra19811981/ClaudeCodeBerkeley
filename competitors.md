# Competitors to Research — URLs for WebFetch

*For Exercise 2. The exercise asks you to use Claude Code's WebFetch tool
to pull each competitor's most recent public statements about their AI
strategy, and to compare their positioning against Meridian's options.*

We focus on four direct competitors. All are publicly traded; all maintain
investor-relations and press pages; all have been visibly active in the
agentic-AI conversation over the last 12 months.

## How to use this file

In your Claude Code prompt, ask Claude to read this file and then to
WebFetch each URL listed below. Specifically:

> Use the WebFetch tool to read the URLs in `competitors.md`. For each
> competitor, extract their most recent public statements about their AI
> strategy — what are they declaring themselves to be? What's their
> agentic posture? Summarize each in one paragraph.

If a URL fails (network, site change, rate limit), try the next URL listed
for the same competitor. If all fail, fall back to `competitors_cached/`
which contains pre-extracted summaries.

## What to look for in each

For each competitor, your goal is one paragraph that answers:

1. **What do they say they are on AI?** ("We are an AI-first work platform"
   vs. "AI is a feature in our product" vs. something else.)
2. **What's their pricing posture?** Bundled? Add-on? Consumption?
3. **What's their flagship AI announcement in the last 6 months?**
4. **Where do they appear positioned vs. Meridian** — same camp, opposite
   camp, or somewhere different?

Then ask Claude: which competitor's positioning is closest to each of
Meridian's two options (PM-with-AI vs. agentic platform)? Where is the
white space?
